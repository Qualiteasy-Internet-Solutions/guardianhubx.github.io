1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are used to improve the user experience and to collect information about browsing behavior.

2. Types of cookies used

Necessary or functional cookies: essential for the proper functioning of the website (e.g., remembering your cookie choice or a partner’s referral code). These are installed without requiring express consent.

Statistics (analytics) cookies: used through Google Analytics to collect aggregated information about site usage and to optimize navigation. These require your prior consent.

Marketing/tracking cookies: we do not currently use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies on guardianhubx.com. If these were to be introduced in the future, the user’s prior consent would be requested and this policy would be updated.

Social media cookies: the site may include links or embedded content from platforms such as YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram or TikTok, which may set their own cookies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of those networks.

5. Cookie settings

The first time you access our website, you are shown a cookie banner with three options, with equal visual prominence for accepting and rejecting, in line with the criteria of the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD):

  • “Reject all”: only the essential technical/functional cookies are installed.
  • “Accept all”: statistics cookies are also installed.
  • “Customize”: lets you enable or disable each category individually.

You can also configure or delete cookies at any time from your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Internet Explorer
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari

Please note the site may not work properly if you disable all cookies; if you delete them, they will be reinstalled once you give your consent again.

6. Retention period

First-party cookies are used only for as long as necessary to fulfill their purpose, not exceeding the periods listed in the table in section 3. For third-party cookies, retention periods are governed by the relevant third party’s privacy policy.
