### How long do we keep your data?

Personal data will be kept for as long as the business or contractual relationship with the data subject is maintained and, once this has ended, for the legally required periods to address any liabilities arising from the processing (generally, up to 5 years for contractual/billing relationships). In other cases, data will be kept until the data subject requests its deletion or withdraws consent.

Qualiteasy Internet Solutions, S.L. periodically reviews data retention periods, deleting on its own initiative any data that has become obsolete or outdated.

### What is the legal basis for processing your data?

The legal basis for processing your data is, depending on the case: performance of a contract or commercial offer, consent given when completing the website's forms, and the Controller's legitimate interest in responding to inquiries related to its products and services.

The following service providers, acting as data processors, will have access to your data as necessary for our activity: hosting and email provider (Google Workspace) and web analytics tool (Google Analytics). Any person has the right to request access to their personal data, its rectification or deletion, the restriction of its processing, to object to processing, as well as the right to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To do so, you may send a letter to Qualiteasy Internet Solutions, S.L. at C/ Pit Roig, 26, 3rd floor, Door B, 08800 Vilanova i la Geltrú (Barcelona), or an email to [qualiteasy@qualiteasy.com](mailto:qualiteasy@qualiteasy.com), stating the reference "GDPR Rights" and the right you wish to exercise.

When exercising your rights, you may be asked to provide sufficient documentation to verify your identity if there is any doubt.

You also have the right to file a complaint with the relevant supervisory authority (Spanish Data Protection Agency, [www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)) if you believe there has been a violation in connection with the processing of your personal data.

If you request restriction of processing, your data will only be kept for the exercise or defense of claims; if you object to processing, it will no longer be processed except for compelling legitimate grounds or the exercise or defense of possible claims.
